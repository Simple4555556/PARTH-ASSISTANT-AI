# Examination Guidelines & Promotion Criteria

**Document ID**: DOC-POL-002  
**Category**: Academic Regulations  
**Effective Date**: Academic Year 2026-2027  
**Visibility**: STUDENT, PARENT, TEACHER, PRINCIPAL  
**Source**: Academic Handbook 2026, Section 4.2, Page 24  

## 1. Exam Eligibility Rules
- Students must have settled all term fees and maintain the minimum mandatory attendance of 75% prior to hall ticket issuance.
- Admit cards are issued digitally on the school portal 7 calendar days prior to the commencement of term exams.

## 2. Passing Marks and Grading Scale
- The minimum passing percentage in every written theory paper is **35%**, and **40%** in combined practical and internal assessments.
- Letter grades are awarded as:
  - A1: 91% to 100% (Outstanding)
  - A2: 81% to 90% (Excellent)
  - B1: 71% to 80% (Very Good)
  - B2: 61% to 70% (Good)
  - C1: 51% to 60% (Satisfactory)
  - C2: 41% to 50% (Acceptable)
  - D: 35% to 40% (Marginal Pass)
  - E: Below 35% (Needs Improvement / Re-test Required)

## 3. Re-examination and Make-Up Tests
Re-examinations are only scheduled for candidates with officially approved medical leave verified by the Examination Committee. Re-tests take place within 14 days of term exam conclusion.
