# School Attendance Policy & Minimum Criteria

**Document ID**: DOC-POL-001  
**Category**: School Policy  
**Effective Date**: Academic Year 2026-2027  
**Visibility**: STUDENT, PARENT, TEACHER, PRINCIPAL  
**Source**: School Handbook 2026, Section 3.1, Page 12  

## 1. Minimum Mandatory Attendance
All students enrolled in Grades 1 through 12 must maintain a minimum overall attendance of **75.0%** across all academic working days in an academic term to be eligible to sit for midterm and final term examinations.

## 2. Medical & Emergency Condonation
- If attendance falls between **60.0% and 74.9%** due to certified medical illness or approved bereavement, the Principal may grant a condonation upon receipt of a registered medical certificate submitted within 3 working days of absence.
- Attendance strictly below **60.0%** is not condonable under any circumstances and requires repetition of the academic term or board review.

## 3. Sports and Co-Curricular Duty Leaves
Students representing the school in official state, national, or inter-school competitions are granted **Duty Leave (DL)**, which is counted as attendance for all eligibility calculations.

## 4. Subject-Wise Attendance Threshold
Students must maintain at least **70.0%** individual attendance in core lab practical subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science).
