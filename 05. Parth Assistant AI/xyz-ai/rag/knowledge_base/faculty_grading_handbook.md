# Faculty Internal Assessment & Moderation Handbook

**Document ID**: DOC-STAFF-001  
**Category**: Staff Operational Guidelines  
**Effective Date**: Academic Year 2026-2027  
**Visibility**: TEACHER, PRINCIPAL  
**Source**: Faculty Manual 2026, Section 6.1, Page 40  

## 1. Internal Assessment Mark Upload Schedule
Teachers must upload periodic test marks to the school ERP within 5 working days following the test date. Term moderation meetings are convened bi-annually.

## 2. Attendance Moderation Authority
Subject teachers are authorized to recommend condonation up to 5% for students actively participating in lab projects or subject exhibitions.

## 3. Discrepancy Escalation
Grade discrepancies flagged by parents must be resolved in consultation with the Department Head within 48 hours.
