# School Fee Schedule & Refund Regulations

**Document ID**: DOC-POL-004  
**Category**: Accounts & Finance  
**Effective Date**: Academic Year 2026-2027  
**Visibility**: STUDENT, PARENT, TEACHER, PRINCIPAL  
**Source**: Financial Regulations Guide 2026, Section 1.3, Page 5  

## 1. Term Fee Payment Deadlines
- Term 1 Fees: Payable on or before April 15th.
- Term 2 Fees: Payable on or before August 15th.
- Term 3 Fees: Payable on or before December 15th.
- A grace period of 10 calendar days is allowed without late penalty.

## 2. Late Surcharge
A nominal late fee of ₹50 per day is applied after the grace period expiration up to a maximum surcharge of ₹1,500 per term.

## 3. Withdrawal and Fee Refund Policy
- If admission withdrawal is formally requested prior to term commencement, 90% of the tuition fee is refundable.
- If withdrawal occurs within 30 days of term commencement, 50% of the tuition fee is refundable.
- Registration and admission processing fees are non-refundable.
