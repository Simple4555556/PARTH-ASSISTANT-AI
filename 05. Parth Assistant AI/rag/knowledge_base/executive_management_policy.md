# Executive Board & Principal Governance Protocol

**Document ID**: DOC-EXEC-001  
**Category**: Executive Governance  
**Effective Date**: Academic Year 2026-2027  
**Visibility**: PRINCIPAL  
**Source**: Board of Trustees Governance Protocol 2026, Section 9.0, Page 55  

## 1. School Budget Allocation Discretion
The Principal holds discretionary authorization for campus infrastructure expenditures up to ₹5,00,000 per fiscal quarter without prior Board convene.

## 2. Emergency School Closure Authority
The Principal is exclusively empowered to declare emergency campus closures in response to severe weather advisories or municipal directives.

## 3. Faculty Appointment & Performance Appraisal
Annual faculty promotions and confidential performance increments are reviewed and approved exclusively by the Principal and Management Committee.
