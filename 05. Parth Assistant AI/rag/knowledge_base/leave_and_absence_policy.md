# Student Leave & Absence Application Policy

**Document ID**: DOC-POL-003  
**Category**: Student Affairs  
**Effective Date**: Academic Year 2026-2027  
**Visibility**: STUDENT, PARENT, TEACHER, PRINCIPAL  
**Source**: School Almanac 2026, Section 2.4, Page 8  

## 1. Planned Leave Application
Parents must submit formal leave requests through the Parent Portal or Parth Assistant AI at least 24 hours prior to the date of absence for planned leaves exceeding 1 day.

## 2. Emergency & Medical Leave
For unplanned absences due to sudden illness, the parent must notify the class teacher via Parth Assistant AI on the morning of the absence. For leaves exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical fitness certificate is mandatory upon return.

## 3. Unexcused Absences & Warning Triggers
Continuous unexcused absence of 5 consecutive working days triggers an automated SMS and email alert to registered parent contacts. Absence exceeding 14 continuous days without notification leads to student status review.
